Post-procedure conduction abnormalities (CA) remain a common complication of transcatheter aortic valve im-
plantation (TAVI), highlighting the need for personalized prediction models. We used machine learning (ML), inte-
grating statistical and mechanistic modelling to provide a patient-speciﬁc estimation of the probability of developing
CA after TAVI.
