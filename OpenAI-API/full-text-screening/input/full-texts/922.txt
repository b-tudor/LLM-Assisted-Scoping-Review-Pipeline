Identifying heart failure (HF) patients who will benefit from cardiac resynchronization therapy (CRT) remains challenging. 
We evaluated whether virtual pacing in a digital twin (DT) of the patient’s heart could be used to predict the degree of 
left ventricular (LV) reverse remodelling post-CRT.
