Abstract: Its use of historical and real-time data and
algorithmic models to digitally create virtual models of physical
entities to simulate, validate, predict, and control the full lifecycle
processes of physical entities is widely acknowledged as a digital
twin. A digital twin is essentially a digital replica of a physical
entity or process. With the advancement of data mining and
digital twin technology, digital twin combined with medical
information data mining is gaining popularity, and this paper
conducts corresponding research based on data mining and
digital twin technology. First, the sensors on the patient are read.
Second, the sensor data is extracted from EEG data features.
Finally the patient's medical history data is data mined from the
SEER database, primarily for specific cancer patients, and an
overall assessment of the patient's medical health status and
feedback to the patient is provided through the integration of the
above data.
Keywords-component; Digital Twins; Smalt Healthcare; Data
